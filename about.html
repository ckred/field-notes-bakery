our story

it started with
a macaron

I started making macarons back in 2013.

Mostly out of curiosity. I’d tried one for the first time and loved it. Back then, it felt like I only ever saw the same few flavors — and there seemed to be so much more potential.

So I started experimenting.

Partly because I wanted to see if I could figure them out. And I did.

There was something like a secret superpower in being able to make these rare little treats for friends and colleagues. I’d bring them to a family Christmas celebration or a birthday party. I even made a great first impression bringing them when I met my in-laws for the first time.

Been downhill ever since… 😂
where it all began
then something shifted

For a long time, that’s all it was.

A half-relationship with baking that seemed to start from scratch every time I had a new oven — or it had simply been a few months since my last bake.

Sometime last year, I wondered what would happen if I took it one step more seriously.

Baked at least once every two weeks.
Took notes.
Paid attention.

Around the same time, we’d been cultivating a garden filled with different herbs and fruits, and started freeze-drying our own and locally grown produce.

Suddenly, there were little curiosities and moments of inspiration everywhere.

I found myself writing those little moments down. Eventually, they’d turn into flavors.

Kalamansi growing
in the garden.

Collecting eggs
from our chickens.

Fresh strawberries we picked
from a local farm.

The smell of jasmine and
honeysuckle from a recent trip.

field notes

I think that’s what Field Notes is.

Just paying attention.
Making something with my hands.
And sharing the parts that can bring a little joy to other people.
