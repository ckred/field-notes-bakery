New field notes are released about every two weeks. Follow along on Instagram for kitchen notes and upcoming batches.

Field Notes is a tiny home bakery in Martinsville, New Jersey. I make macarons slowly and mostly by hand, using eggs from our chickens, fruit and other ingredients I prepare myself, and the best ingredients I can find.

I work with real artists, make things in quantities that still allow me to care about them, and leave room for a little intuition along the way.

The same attention that shapes my Reiki practice at Diwa finds its way into this work, too.
