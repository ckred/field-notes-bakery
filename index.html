New field notes are released about every four weeks. Follow along on Instagram for kitchen notes and upcoming batches.

Field Notes is a tiny home bakery in Martinsville, New Jersey. I make macarons slowly and by hand, using eggs from our chickens, fruits and flowers from our garden, and the best ingredients I can find.

I like to be intentional with the energy that goes into creating and enjoying food, so I practice an intention setting with each batch of macarons, wishing care and wellbeing for each person who receives them.
